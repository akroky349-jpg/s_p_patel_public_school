Co-Curriculars & Student Life

Nurturing passion, sportsmanship, artistic creativity, and leadership beyond textbooks.

Holistic Spectrum

Enriching Activities for Every Scholar

A comprehensive array of clubs, athletic events, artistic studios, and intellectual forums.

Sports & Fitness

Athletics, Cricket & Football

Inter-house athletic meets, 100m/200m/400m sprint relays, long jump, cricket tournaments, volleyball leagues, and daily physical fitness drills.

Performing Arts

Classical Dance, Music & Choir

Celebration of Indian classical dance (Kathak, Bharatnatyam), vocal Indian ragas, school orchestra, patriotic anthems, and festival pageants.

Grand Celebrations

Annual Function & Prize Distribution

The pinnacle of our school calendar: theatrical dramas, musical performances, academic laurels, and prestigious award distributions attended by parents and dignitaries.

Creative Arts

Fine Arts, Sketching & Clay Craft

Watercolor landscapes, oil pastels, calligraphy, origami, pottery modeling, and recycled-waste sculpture nurturing imaginative visual aesthetics.

STEM & Innovation

Annual Science & Innovation Expo

Working scientific models, solar energy prototypes, hydraulic lifts, automation circuits, environmental dioramas, and mathematics exhibits built by scholars.

Oratory & Mind

Debate, Quiz Bowl & Elocution

Inter-school debate forums, Sanskrit shloka chanting, Hindi kavita path, general knowledge quiz championships, spelling bees, and chess tournaments.

Student Leadership

The Four-House Spirit & Governance

To foster healthy competition, collective belonging, and peer mentorship, our student body is organized into four distinct institutional houses.

Each house is steered by student Captains and Vice-Captains under faculty House Masters. Throughout the academic term, houses compete across athletic tracks, academic Olympiads, theatrical presentations, and cleanliness drives for the prestigious Annual Rolling Trophy.

Tagore House Ashoka House Raman House Shivaji House

Values Nurtured Through Co-Curriculars

  • • Team Spirit: Learning to subordinate individual ego for the collective triumph of the group.
  • • Grace in Defeat: Applauding victors, learning from shortcomings, and returning stronger.
  • • Stage Poise: Shedding inhibition, projecting voice, and addressing large audiences with confidence.
  • • Time Management: Balancing scholastic studies with rigorous athletic and cultural commitments.
