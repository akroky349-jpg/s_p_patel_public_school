Welcoming New Scholars

An Inviting Pathway to Quality Education

We appreciate your interest in S. P. Patel Public School. Our admissions process is designed to be fair, transparent, and parent-friendly.

We seek enthusiastic learners eager to embrace academic challenge, creative arts, and disciplined character building. Admission is offered on merit, seat availability, and candidate readiness, without discrimination based on caste, creed, or community.

Registrations for the upcoming academic session are currently accepted for Nursery through Class 12. We encourage early application to secure preferred enrollment.

Clear Steps

Step-by-Step Admission Process

Five simple stages to welcome your child into our school community.

1

Online / Campus Enquiry

Submit the online admission enquiry form or visit the school admissions counter in Bhanauri to obtain prospectus.

2

Form Submission & Docs

Fill in student biographical information and submit self-attested copies of birth certificate, previous report card, and photos.

3

Interaction / Assessment

Friendly oral interaction for Pre-Primary; baseline diagnostic assessment in English & Math for primary to senior grades.

4

Confirmation & Fee

Upon confirmation, complete the admission fee deposit and collect the welcome orientation dossier and booklist.

Age Criteria

Grade-Wise Eligibility Matrix

Recommended age benchmarks as of 31st March of the admission academic year.

Class / Grade Minimum Age (as on 31st March) Prerequisite & Evaluation
Nursery 3+ Years Informal child-parent interaction; basic motor recognition.
LKG (Lower Kindergarten) 4+ Years Basic verbal communication, color, and object identification.
UKG (Upper Kindergarten) 5+ Years Alphabet tracing, phonetic sounds, and numbers 1 to 20.
Class 1 6+ Years Completion of pre-primary schooling; basic reading & writing.
Classes 2 to 5 (Primary) 7+ to 10+ Years Transfer Certificate (TC) & Marksheet of preceding grade.
Classes 6 to 8 (Middle) 11+ to 13+ Years TC from recognized school + Diagnostic aptitude check.
Classes 9 & 11 (Secondary & Sr. Sec.) 14+ to 16+ Years Class 8 / Class 10 board score card, TC, Migration & Stream interview.
Documentation

Required Documents Checklist

Please ensure the following verified documents are ready at the time of admission.

For Pre-Primary to Class 1

  • Original & photocopy of Municipal Birth Certificate.
  • Recent passport-size colour photographs of the student (4 copies).
  • Photographs of parents / guardian (2 copies each).
  • Aadhaar Card copy of the child and parents.
  • Child immunization / medical record card copy.

For Classes 2 to 12 (Transfer Cases)

  • Original Transfer Certificate (TC) countersigned by Education Authority / Board.
  • Self-attested copy of the preceding year's Report Card / Marksheet.
  • Passport-size photographs of the student and parents.
  • Aadhaar Card copy & Proof of Residence (Electricity bill, Ration card, Voter ID).
  • Class 10 Board Registration / Pass Certificate (for Class 11 & 12 entrants).
