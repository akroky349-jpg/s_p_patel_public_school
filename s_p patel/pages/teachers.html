Faculty Development & Pedagogy Workshops

Great schools are built upon great teachers. At S. P. Patel Public School, professional training is an ongoing institutional commitment.

Throughout the academic year, our faculty participates in pedagogical enrichment seminars, child psychology symposiums, smart classroom technology updates, and national curriculum framework workshops. This ensures our educators remain abreast of best instructional practices.
