World-Class Campus Facilities

Modern infrastructure designed to stimulate academic passion, scientific inquiry, athletic health, and creative expression.

Verified Infrastructure

An Inspiring Setting for Holistic Growth

Every square foot of S. P. Patel Public School, Bhanauri, Prayagraj is purpose-built to ensure safety, ergonomic comfort, and state-of-the-art educational support.

Learning Spaces

Smart Interactive Classrooms

Our classrooms are spacious, naturally illuminated, and cross-ventilated. Outfitted with interactive digital touch displays, multimedia projectors, and ergonomic child-friendly seating, each classroom transforms textbook theory into vivid visual discovery.

Research & Inquiry

Composite Science Laboratories

Fully equipped Physics, Chemistry, and Biology laboratory wings furnished with precision optical microscopes, chemical apparatus, specimen cabinets, and fire safety systems. Supervised experiments empower scholars to explore natural sciences with confidence.

Digital Literacy

Advanced Computer & AI Lab

A dedicated computer center equipped with high-speed networked desktop computers, licensed programming suites, modern operating systems, and filtered internet. Students learn coding, digital design, and computational thinking from early grades onward.

Knowledge Hub

Knowledge Resource Centre (Library)

Our library holds thousands of academic reference books, encyclopedia volumes, classic literature in English and Hindi, periodicals, and educational magazines. Quiet reading bays and reading lamps provide a peaceful atmosphere for intellectual exploration.

Physical Wellness

Expansive Sports Complex & Grounds

Extensive open-air sports fields featuring standard athletic running tracks, turf cricket pitch, football field, volleyball court, badminton courts, and dedicated yoga areas. Guided by physical education masters, students cultivate stamina, discipline, and teamwork.

Safe Commute

GPS-Tracked School Transport Fleet

A well-maintained fleet of regulatory yellow school buses covering principal routes across Bhanauri, Belsara, and surrounding sectors of Prayagraj. Each vehicle is equipped with GPS tracking, speed governors, first aid kits, and verified drivers and attendants.

Health & Care

Health Infirmary & First Aid

A dedicated, hygienic medical room equipped with patient examination beds, basic diagnostic tools, first-aid pharmaceuticals, and tie-ups with nearby healthcare centers for prompt medical attention. Regular health and vision screening camps are organized.

Hygiene & Safety

Pure RO Drinking Water & Sanitation

Multi-stage reverse osmosis (RO) drinking water filtration plants installed across each campus floor with regular microbiological quality audits. Separate, meticulously sanitised restrooms are maintained for boys, girls, and staff members.

Uncompromising Safety

Campus Security & Vigilance

The safety of our students is our paramount institutional priority. Our campus is guarded by round-the-clock trained security personnel, high-definition closed-circuit television (CCTV) coverage at all entry/exit gates and corridors, strict visitor log protocols, and certified fire safety installations.
